You knew the meeting was at 11. You checked the clock at 10:55. The next time you looked, it was 11:07.

Calendar notifications are a small grey box in the corner, and your brain learned to ignore those years ago. Hey You! is the reminder you can't ignore: 20 seconds before every meeting on your Google Calendar, every screen you own turns red, with the meeting name, a countdown and one big Join button.

Press Enter and you're in the call. Click anywhere or press Esc and the alert is gone. It never locks your Mac.

WHY 20 SECONDS
A 10-minute reminder gets a "yeah, in a bit", and then you forget again. At 20 seconds there is nothing left to finish, so you join.

WHAT IT DOES
• Takes over every display, and shows above full-screen apps too
• Finds the Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Webex link and opens it when you press Enter
• Stays on screen until you react or the meeting ends, instead of sliding away after five seconds
• Tells you how late you are if your Mac was asleep: "Started 3 min ago"
• Ignores the keys you were already typing, so you can't dismiss it by accident
• Lives in the menu bar with today's meetings. It has no Dock icon and no windows to manage
• Has a Test alert in the menu, so the first real one doesn't catch you by surprise

YOUR CALENDAR STAYS ON YOUR MAC
• Read-only Google sign-in. Hey You! can't change or delete anything
• Events go straight from Google to your Mac. There are no Hey You! servers and no Hey You! account
• Usage statistics are optional and never include meeting titles, links or attendees

FREE FOR THE JOB THAT MATTERS
The free version alerts you 20 seconds before every timed meeting, with no time limit and no trial.

HEY YOU! PRO
A one-time purchase, with no subscription, for people who want to tune it:
• Set the alert anywhere from 1 second to 2 minutes before
• Skip meetings you declined
• Skip focus blocks where you are the only attendee
• Only alert for meetings that have a video link
• Choose which calendars count, and your working hours
• Pick the sound and see a countdown in the menu bar
• See your week: "On time 11 of 12 this week"

Requires a Google Calendar account and macOS 14 or later. English and Spanish.
